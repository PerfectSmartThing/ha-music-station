# Music Station

Music Assistant + karaoke lyrics in a single Home Assistant card.

- Embeds the Music Assistant add-on UI (auto-detected, ingress handled for you)
- Synced lyrics from LRCLIB with a karaoke-style highlight
- Player picker (search, remembers your choice) and automatic fallback to the player that has lyrics
- Album art through the HA media proxy (no mixed-content issues)

```yaml
type: custom:music-station
```
